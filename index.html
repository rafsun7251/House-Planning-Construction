What We Do

Complete Architectural & Civil Consultancy

From digital land survey to 3D elevation rendering and structural safety certifications under BNBC regulations.

2D Architectural Floor Planning

Optimized room layouts, natural light & air ventilation flow, staircase placement, furniture placement, and RAJUK / Municipality approval drawings.

3D Exterior & Interior Renderings

Photorealistic 3D front elevation models, color matching, modern wooden & tile facades, landscaping, and virtual walkthrough animations.

BNBC Structural Engineering

Earthquake-resistant RCC column, beam, footing, foundation load calculations by certified IEB Member Civil Engineers.

Plumbing & Electrical Layouts

Complete pipe diameter schematics, septictank & water underground reservoir design, circuit breaker capacity, and lighting points.

Construction Cost & BOQ Estimation

Detailed material list (Cement, Steel, Bricks, Sand, Tiles, Paint) and item-by-item labor cost budget estimation to prevent overspending.

On-Site Construction Supervision

Engineers visit your site during key construction phases (Footing casting, Column casting, Roof slab casting) to ensure strict quality control.
